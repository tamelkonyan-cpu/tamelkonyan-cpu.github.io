EC 473 / 573

Games and Decisions

An applications-oriented course in game theory: extensive and normal form representations, beliefs and mixed strategies, dominance and rationalizability, Nash equilibrium, backward induction and subgame perfection, and bargaining, with examples drawn from economics, business, politics and law. Offered at both the advanced undergraduate and graduate level.

Enrolled students should use Blackboard for syllabi, slides and problem sets. For questions about a course, email me or come to office hours in 251 Alston Hall.
